bool PolyhedralEB::isFluid ( const Real  a_value)
inlinenoexcept

Which side of the interface a value lies on.

One predicate for corners and crossings alike: negative is fluid, and anything else, an exact zero of either sign included, is solid. GeometryShop reads an exact zero by its sign bit, which a negated distance function turns over, and then rescues the regular cell that leaves beside a covered one by looking at its neighbour. A cell here is classified from its own corners alone, so the zero has to fall on one side by rule: on the solid side, so that a fluid cell whose face lies in the interface is cut with that face as its interface, and the cell on the other side is covered.

True on the fluid side.
