Detailed Description

One patch's identity for the duration of a merge round: its AMR level, local DataIndex, and its own level's cell spacing.

Shared by every backend (mergeNearestNeighborsTree()/mergeNearestNeighborsOneCell()) as the parallel index into their own per-patch vectors (patchLocalValid/patchGhosts/...).
