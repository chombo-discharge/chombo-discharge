Detailed Description

The hybrid divergence with flux redistribution, as an EBRedistribution.

In a cut cell with volume fraction kappa, the right-hand side is the hybrid divergence

R = kappa*D + (1-kappa)*D_nc,

where D is the conservative divergence and D_nc the non-conservative divergence, a kappa-weighted average of kappa*D over the neighbouring cells. The mass that this misses, (1-kappa)*(kappa*D - kappa*D_nc), is redistributed to the neighbouring cells through the EBFluxRedistribution operators of the AmrMesh, on this level and across refinement boundaries. The result depends on neither the state nor the time step.
