Detailed Description

The polyhedral graph as an AMR hierarchy of any layout holds it: every cell of every box, ghost cells included, described by the geometry level that owns it.

The geometry's graph lives on its own levels and its own tiles. A consumer lives on its own hierarchy – one DisjointBoxLayout per level, each box filled to a ghost width, less what the next finer level covers – and a cell of it may be owned by the geometry level at its own resolution, by a finer one, or by a coarser one. Each box keeps the part of the graph that intersects it and nothing else: per geometry level, the pieces it received – boxes of cells one tile of that level owns, with their states – and the surfaces and face overrides of the cells among them that hold any. Nothing here is recomputed from the implicit function; PolyhedralGeometryShop::copyGraphs fills it from the graphs alone.

A consumer asks a destination cell for the cut cells that intersect it, getCutCells, each as a CutCellDescription from which CutCellBody::define builds its polyhedron, and asks getState what a cell with none is.

◆ finish()

void AMRPolyhedralEBGraph::finish ( )

Put every box's surfaces in cell order and index its pieces, after the last addPiece and addSurface and before anything is read.

The index is a coarse grid over each box's pieces, of bins as wide as its widest piece, each listing the pieces that reach it: a cell is found among the few pieces of its bin rather than among all of a box's pieces, which a coarse destination box gathering a finer level's tiles has many of.

void AMRPolyhedralEBGraph::getCutCells ( std::vector< PolyhedralEB::CutCellDescription > &  a_cells,
const int  a_level,

The cut cells of the geometry that intersect a cell of a destination box, each with everything its polyhedron is built from.

A cell the geometry owns at the destination's resolution is its own cut cell; one owned by a finer level is intersected by every cut cell of that level under it; one owned by a coarser level is intersected by the cut ancestor, whose polyhedron the consumer subdivides down to the cell. Each comes with its face overrides. A cell none of whose owners is cut gives none; getState says what it is.

Parameters
[out]a_cellsThe cut cells, appended to an emptied list.
[in]a_levelDestination level.
[in]a_ditDestination box.
[in]a_cellThe cell, on the destination level, in the box or its ghost cells.
