Detailed Description

One reply to a single claim: "your specific box nominally won or lost this member" – the wire record for the carve protocol's Phase 2 (verdicts routed back to proposers, foreign members only).

Trivially copyable (see KDClaim). Answers exactly the claim identified by (memberID, claimantBoxIdx) – one KDVerdict per incoming KDClaim, not one per rank, so that two of the SAME rank's own boxes claiming the same particle each get their own, individually correct answer (see KDClaim::proposerBoxIdx). NOMINAL, not final: winning the argmin for one member does not by itself guarantee the winning box will actually commit – it can independently lose OTHER members to OTHER, unrelated lower-keyed competitors and end up under the merge threshold itself. An owner must NOT delete a member on the strength of this verdict alone – it must wait for the corresponding KDCommit in Phase 3. See mergeKDCarve()'s own docs for a worked counterexample of the conservation bug that results from deleting here instead.
