Detailed Description

Base class for the boundary conditions on the EB faces of cut cells that are not interface cells (electrodes).

The flux through the EB face of a cut cell is returned as a linear form in the cell values and the boundary data g,

F = w_B * g + sum_j w_j * phi_j,

where F is the normal derivative of phi with n pointing out of the cell, and the stencil is over PETSc rows, as in PetscHelmholtzEBStencils. F does not contain the b-coefficient or the face area, which PetscHelmholtzOp multiplies in. The stencil goes into the matrix, and w_B * g into the right-hand side. This mirrors PetscHelmholtzDomainBC for the domain faces.
