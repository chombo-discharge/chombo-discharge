Detailed Description

AMR driver that deposits/interpolates ParticleContainer particles across the hierarchy.

EBAMRParticleMesh deposits the particles of a ParticleContainer onto AMR mesh data and interpolates mesh data back onto particle columns, by driving the per-patch EBParticleMesh leaf on every box of every level. define() builds the per-level leaves, the level valid+ghost -> valid Copiers, the EBCoarseFineParticleMesh operators, and the outer-halo masks; it must be called before any deposit or interpolate.

Interpolation

interpolate() and interpolateWeight() are purely per-level/per-patch: the mesh is assumed to be valid+ghosted by the caller, so no coarse-fine coupling is needed.

Deposition

deposit(), depositWeight(), and depositGathered() conserve mass across refinement boundaries, and select how the coarse-fine overlap is handled through CoarseFineDeposition:

  • Interp – move the over-boundary mass between levels with the coarse-fine mesh operators.
  • Halo – copy the coarse-side halo particles into the finer patches their clouds reach, as coarse ghost particles, and deposit them there with the coarse width.
  • HaloNGP – deposit the near-boundary halo particles with NGP so their clouds do not spread across the boundary.
  • Transition – deposit the coarse-side transition particles with the fine-grid width, on their own level and as coarse ghost particles on the finer one. Halo, HaloNGP, and Transition require at least one ghost cell. Halo and Transition need the realm's coarse-to-fine particle ghost masks (see getCoarseToFineMaskWidths).

Cut cells, and what this class does NOT do

The deposition routines take an IrregularDeposition, but this class only implements the part of it that the leaf kernels can answer: NGP in a cut cell, yes or no. Everything else is the CALLER's responsibility.

  • Native, NGP – handled here. These are the two kernel behaviours.
  • Mirror – NOT handled here. It is a second pass that reflects each band particle across the embedded boundary and deposits the image, so it needs an image container, a scratch field and a collective remap, none of which this class owns. See ItoSolver::mirrorPass. The one part handled here: Interp's coarse-to-fine step does not rescale cut cells by volume fraction, because with the mirror they hold the density rather than kappa times it.
  • Redistribute, RedistributeBlended – NOT handled here. They act on the field AFTER the deposit. See ItoSolver::redistributeAMR and McPhoto::depositHybridDivergence.

The consequence is worth stating plainly, because it is silent: passing Mirror or Redistribute to a deposition routine here and then omitting the caller's half produces Native behaviour (for Mirror with Interp, Native without the volume-fraction rescaling), with no error and no diagnostic. A caller that selects one of those values owns the rest of its meaning.

This split is deliberate but not ideal – the enum is named in one place and interpreted in three, so there is no single source of truth for what a selector means. Centralizing the mirror and the redistribution here is the natural end state; it needs ParticleContainer::define's minimum block size, level tiles, valid cells and realm name, which this class is not currently given.

Build the per-width outer-halo masks: per level, the coarse cells just outside the fine patch.

For each ghost width in [1, m_ghost] and each level with a finer level, marks the coarse cells within that width of the refinement boundary (on the coarse side), excluding the coarsened-fine valid region and its neighbours. The mask drives copyMaskParticles in the Halo deposit. Storage-agnostic (operates on the grids only). Empty BaseFabs are undefined.

The CoarseFineDeposition::Halo deposition algorithm.

Like Interp for the on-level deposit and the fine-ghost transfer to the coarse level, but instead of interpolating the coarse mass under the fine grid, the coarse-side halo particles (width-1 outer-halo mask) are copied into the finer patches as coarse ghosts and deposit there with the coarse width, clipped to the valid box. Their coarse-level deposit is unchanged; its part under the finer grid is invalid.

The CoarseFineDeposition::HaloNGP deposition algorithm.

Moves the coarse-side halo particles (width-1 mask) out of the valid holder, deposits the remaining (non-halo) particles with the requested kernel and the halo particles with NGP onto the same level mesh, folds fine-ghost mass to the coarse level, then transfers the halo particles back to the valid holder. NGP keeps the near-boundary clouds from spreading over the refinement boundary.

The CoarseFineDeposition::Transition deposition algorithm.

NGP short-circuits to the Interp result. Otherwise, the coarser particles within the transition band of a finer grid deposit with the finer-level width: on their own level, into the same data as the other particles, and as coarse ghosts in the finer patches their clouds reach, clipped to the valid box. The part of the coarse-level deposit under the finer grid is invalid, so the composite mass is conserved.
