Detailed Description

Shared one-sided EB face stencils for the EB and jump boundary conditions of PetscHelmholtzOp.

For each cut cell of each phase, this class holds the one-sided normal derivative of phi at the EB face,

dphi/dn = w_B * phi_B + sum_i w_i * phi_i,

where phi_B is the value on the EB face, n points out of the cell (into the boundary, i.e. minus the EB normal), and the stencil is over PETSc rows of the cell's phase. phi is cell-centered.

The stencil is a weighted least-squares fit at the EB face centroid, with phi_B known, over the cells in a neighbourhood of the cut cell on its own level, within a radius equal to the order:

  • Cells that are PETSc rows are used as they are.
  • Coarse-fine ghost cells are used as well, and are replaced by their interpolation stencils from the EBMultigridInterpolator (PetscGrid::getPetscStencilWithGhostCells), which are over rows on this level and the next coarser one. The interpolator fills the ghost cells within its radius of a cut cell, so the order must not exceed that radius.
  • Cells covered by the next finer level are replaced by the finer cells under them that are PETSc rows.

Every cell of the neighbourhood enters the fit, as in FieldSolverGMG. The search tries the half-circle, quadrant and radius neighbourhoods, each from the order down to 1. A fit is accepted only if w_B > 0, i.e. if the normal derivative increases with the boundary value, as in FieldSolverGMG. If no fit is accepted, or if a cut cell has no EB normal, the cell has no flux through its EB face: its stencil is empty with w_B = 0. The stencils are computed once, in define, and contain neither the b-coefficient nor the face area.

PetscHelmholtzEBBC (electrode faces) and PetscHelmholtzJumpBC (interface faces) derive from this class and build their fluxes from these stencils.
