For an interior face, lists the faces between the finer VoFs of its low and high VoFs, pairing them in the order of getFinerVoFs at each factor of two as EBISLayout::refine does. Vector< VolIndex > VofUtils::getFinerVoFs ( const VolIndex & a_coarVoF,
const EBISLayout & a_ebislFine,
const DataIndex & a_din,
const int a_refRat )
static

Get the VoFs on a finer layout that coarsen to a coarse VoF.

Finds the VoFs among the cells of refine(coarse cell, a_refRat) whose coarsening by a_refRat is a_coarVoF. They are listed in the order EBISLayout::refine lists them for a graph that Chombo coarsened: at each factor of two, the VoFs one level finer are gathered in BoxIterator order of their cells, and each connected set is listed in the order it grows from its first VoF, as in EBGraph::getVoFSets. VoFs on intermediate levels are identified through coarsening, and two of them are connected when some pair of their finer VoFs shares a face. Only coarsening links are read, so the coarse graph needs no links to the finer level.

Parameters
[in]a_coarVoFCoarse VoF.
[in]a_ebislFineFiner layout. Shares DataIndex with the layout of a_coarVoF, holds every cell of refine(coarse cell, a_refRat), and has a maximum coarsening ratio of at least a_refRat.
[in]a_dinGrid index on both layouts.
[in]a_refRatRefinement ratio between the two layouts. A power of two.
Returns
The finer VoFs that coarsen to a_coarVoF.

Get the finer faces of a coarse face from stored finer-VoF lists.

When every finer cell of both coarse cells lies in the finer EBISBox, this gives EBISLayout::refine's result: with a refinement ratio of 2 it pairs the finer VoFs of the low and high VoFs that are connected on the finer graph, as EBGraph::refine does, and with a larger ratio it calls getFinerFaces. Otherwise it starts from the coarse cell whose finer cells all lie in the finer EBISBox, and keeps the faces of its finer VoFs next to the face whose other VoF coarsens to the other coarse VoF; this needs one finer ghost cell. A face on the boundary of the coarse region takes the boundary faces of the finer VoFs of its interior VoF.

Parameters
[in]a_coarFaceCoarse face.
[in]a_ebisBoxCoarCoarse EBISBox holding a_coarFace.
[in]a_finerVoFsStored finer-VoF lists of the irregular coarse cells, on the same grid index.
[in]a_ebislFineFiner layout, sharing DataIndex with the coarse layout.
[in]a_dinGrid index on both layouts.
[in]a_refRatRefinement ratio between the coarse and the finer layout.
Returns
The finer faces of a_coarFace.

Get the finer VoFs of a coarse VoF from stored finer-VoF lists.

A regular coarse cell gives every cell of refine(coarse cell, a_refRat) with VoF index 0, in the order EBISLayout::refine gives them: refined by two at a time, each cell's children in BoxIterator order. A covered coarse cell gives no VoFs. An irregular coarse cell gives its stored list, which must exist. The result equals EBISLayout::refine on a graph that Chombo coarsened.

Parameters
[in]a_coarVoFCoarse VoF.
[in]a_ebisBoxCoarCoarse EBISBox holding a_coarVoF.
[in]a_finerVoFsStored finer-VoF lists of the irregular coarse cells, on the same grid index.
[in]a_refRatRefinement ratio between the coarse and the finer layout.
Returns
The finer VoFs of a_coarVoF.

Group the finest VoFs of one VoF by their ancestor one factor of two finer than it, and order the groups.

The groups are ordered as EBGraph::getVoFSets lists the VoFs of one connected set: gathered in BoxIterator order of the ancestors' cells, then cell index, and listed in the order the set grows from its first member. Two groups are connected when some pair of their finest VoFs shares a face.

Returns
The finest VoFs of each group, groups in order.
