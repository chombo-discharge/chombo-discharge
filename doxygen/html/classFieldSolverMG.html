Detailed Description

Field solver that solves with geometric multigrid, and falls back to algebraic multigrid when that fails.

This class composes a FieldSolverGMG and, in builds with PETSc, a FieldSolverAMG. Each solve goes through the geometric multigrid solver chain first (e.g. 'gmg bicgstab'). If that does not converge, the solve is repeated with PETSc's algebraic multigrid, starting from the same initial guess. The algebraic multigrid solver is only set up the first time it is needed after a regrid. Once it has rescued a solve, the following solves go straight to it, and geometric multigrid is re-tried every amg_retry_interval solves.

The two solvers share this solver's data: they read and write its potential, space and surface charge, electric field, residue and permittivities. They also read their settings from this class's input options, so that the boundary conditions, the plot variables, the interface condition and the EB stencils are given once, and agree. The EB stencil order and weight (bc_order, bc_weight) apply to electrodes and dielectric interfaces in both solvers.

Without PETSc, the algebraic multigrid fallback is not compiled, and amg_fallback is ignored with a warning. The fallback is also skipped on periodic domains, and when the EB stencils reach further than the coarse-fine interpolation radius, since algebraic multigrid does not support these.

Geometric and algebraic multigrid assemble the operator differently, so a solve that falls back gives a slightly different discrete solution.
