Detailed Description

Neumann boundary condition dphi/dn = g on the EB faces, for PetscHelmholtzOp.

n points out of the cell, into the EB, as in EBHelmholtzNeumannEBBC. The flux is the given normal derivative, so the stencil is empty and w_B = 1.
