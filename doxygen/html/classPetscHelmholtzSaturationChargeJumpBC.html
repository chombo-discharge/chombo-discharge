Detailed Description

The saturation charge condition D_p = 0 on the interface, on the side of one phase p, for PetscHelmholtzOp.

The closure eliminates phi_B with phi_B = -sum_i w_p,i phi_p,i / w_B,p, so the flux into side p is zero, and the flux into the other side couples the rows of both phases. There is no jump data. The surface charge density is an output: updateJumpData computes it from the solution, sigma = b_0 * D_0 + b_1 * D_1, and keeps it in m_sigma, as MFHelmholtzSaturationChargeJumpBC does.
