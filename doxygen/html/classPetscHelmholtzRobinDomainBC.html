Detailed Description

Robin domain boundary condition A*phi + B*dphi/dn = C for PetscHelmholtzOp.

As in EBHelmholtzRobinDomainBC, phi is extrapolated linearly to the face from the cell and its interior neighbor, and the flux is s*(A*phi_face - C)/B with s = -1 on the low side and +1 on the high side, since n points into the domain. The interior neighbor must be a cell on the same level, i.e. not covered by the EB or by a finer grid. Where it is not, the extrapolation needs a least-squares reconstruction over the valid cells on this level and the next finer level, which is not implemented yet and aborts.
