Detailed Description

A class for moving deposited particle mass between two levels across a refinement boundary. This class does not include any deposition functionality.

Both operations go through the coarsened fine grids:

  1. addFineGhostsToCoarse adds the part of the fine level's particle clouds that hangs over the refinement boundary (the fine ghost cells) to the coarse level.
  2. addInvalidCoarseToFine interpolates the coarse-level deposit under the fine grid to the fine level, for when the coarse-grid clouds deposit under the fine level but the mass should end up on the fine level.

Coarsen the fine-level ghost cells and add them to the coarse level.

On output, the contents in the ghost cells on the refinement boundary are added from the fine level to the coarse level. This routine is used when the fine-level particles have particle clouds that deposit into the ghost cells outside the fine level, i.e. over the refinement boundary. This routine coarsens that data and adds the mass to the coarse level. The data is mass per cell volume, so each coarse VoF gets the sum of the fine ghost VoFs that coarsen to it, divided by refRat^SpaceDim, which conserves mass.

Function which piecewise interpolates the data underneath the fine grid and adds it to the fine grid.

Every valid fine VoF gets the density of the coarse VoF it coarsens to. When the data is mass per cell volume (kappa times the density in cut cells), the coarse value is scaled by the ratio of the fine to the coarse volume fraction in cut cells, which conserves mass. When the cut cells already hold the density, as with mirror deposition, the coarse value is used as is. The coarse valid data is copied onto the coarsened fine grids first, so no geometry finer than the fine level's is needed.
