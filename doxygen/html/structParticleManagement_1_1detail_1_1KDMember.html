Detailed Description

One member of a boundary box, as listed by its proposer: a global id plus that particle's current owning rank.

Eligibility to join a box's final (post-carve) membership is by this explicit listing only, never by raw geometric AABB containment – a box's AABB can enclose a particle its proposer never actually listed.
