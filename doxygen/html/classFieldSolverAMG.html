Detailed Description

Electrostatic field solver that assembles the operator as a PETSc matrix and solves it with a PETSc Krylov method, preconditioned by algebraic multigrid.

This is the PETSc counterpart of FieldSolverGMG, with the same inputs and outputs. The operator is -div(eps * grad(phi)) = rho/eps0, assembled by PetscHelmholtzOp. The operator spans all AMR levels and both phases, with electrodes on the EB (PetscHelmholtzElectrostaticEBBC) and the natural or saturation-charge condition on the interface between the gas and the dielectrics. Periodic domains and multi-valued cells are not supported.

PetscErrorCode FieldSolverAMG::monitorResidual ( KSP a_ksp,

PetscReal a_residualNorm,
void * a_context )
staticprotected

KSP monitor that prints the residual at every iteration, as the Krylov solvers of FieldSolverGMG do.

The residual is in the norm the KSP tests convergence on (the preconditioned residual for left-preconditioned KSPs), relative to that norm at the first iteration. The rate is the reduction factor since the previous iteration. With petsc_verbosity 2 and above the true residual b - A*x is also printed, relative to the zero-residual b.

◆ m_petscVerbosity

int FieldSolverAMG::m_petscVerbosity
protected

Verbosity of the PETSc solve.

At 1 and above, the residual is printed at every Krylov iteration. At 2 and above, the true residual is printed too. At 3 and above, the solver and the preconditioner are viewed after each solve.
