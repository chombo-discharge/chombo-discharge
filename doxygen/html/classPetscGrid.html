Detailed Description

Class which maps a multifluid cut-cell AMR hierarchy to PETSc.

This class includes functions for putting chombo-discharge in PETSc and vice versa.

Note
This class assumes that PETSc will only care about a single component per grid cell (two components in a multifluid cell).

Add the rows of a stencil's cells to a PETSc stencil.

Looks up each entry of a_stencil in a_map. Every entry must lie in a_map's box and be a PETSc row on a_phase. Either failure aborts in every build, through abortInvalidEntry: an entry outside the map would read out of bounds, and PETSc silently ignores negative indices.

◆ getPetscStencil()

PetscStencil PetscGrid::getPetscStencil ( const VoFStencil & a_stencil,
const int a_level,
const DataIndex & a_din,
const int a_phase ) const
virtualnoexcept

Rewrite a stencil over cells on one level as a stencil over global PETSc rows.

Each entry of a_stencil is a cell on level a_level, in the index space of grid a_din, possibly in its ghost region. Every entry must be a PETSc row on a_phase, i.e. a valid cell of a_din or of a neighbouring box. Anything else aborts with the reason: a cell covered by a finer level, a coarse-fine ghost cell, a cell covered by the EB, or a cell outside the domain. Ghost cells and boundary conditions are the caller's to resolve first.

Parameters
[in]a_stencilStencil over cells on level a_level.
[in]a_levelGrid level.
[in]a_dinGrid index on level a_level.
[in]a_phasePhase.
Returns
The same stencil, over global PETSc rows, compressed (unique, ascending rows).

Rewrite a stencil over three levels, whose part on the middle level may contain coarse-fine ghost cells, as a stencil over global PETSc rows.

The ghost cells in a_stencil are replaced as in replaceGhostCells. The parts then resolve as in getPetscStencilWithCoarserLevel and getPetscStencilWithFinerLevel: a_stencilCoar is in the index space of grid a_din coarsened, and a_stencilFine in the index space of grid a_din refined. Aborts if the coarse part is not empty on level 0, or the fine part on the finest level.

[in]a_dinGrid index on level a_level
[in]a_phasePhase

◆ replaceGhostCells()

void PetscGrid::replaceGhostCells ( VoFStencil & a_stencil,
VoFStencil & a_stencilCoar,

const int a_level,

virtualnoexcept

Replace the coarse-fine ghost cells in a stencil over level a_level cells by their interpolation stencils.

Each ghost cell entry is removed from a_stencil, and its stencil from EBMultigridInterpolator::getGhostStencil, times the entry's weight, is added to a_stencil (the part on level a_level) and a_stencilCoar (the part on level a_level - 1, in the index space of grid a_din coarsened). Entries that are not ghost cells are kept. Aborts if a_stencil has a ghost cell and a_interpolator is null.

Parameters

[in,out]a_stencilCoarStencil over cells on level a_level - 1
[in]a_interpolatorCoarse-fine interpolator of level a_level, for the phase of the stencil. Null on level 0.
[in]a_levelGrid level
[in]a_dinGrid index on the level
