Detailed Description

Everything one cut cell of the polyhedral geometry is built from, and which cell it is.

The cell's level and index, its surface – corner values and edge crossings, and the role that says whether its faces are its own – and the face overrides that replace the faces that are not, holding this cell's entry alone. Self-contained: CutCellBody::define(m_surface, m_overrides, m_cell) builds the cell's polyhedron from it, and nothing else is needed. No interior is stored; the polyhedron, its fan of interface triangles included, is built by whoever wants it.
